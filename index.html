> RESEARCH OVERVIEW

Welcome! I am Meilen Salamanca, a Bioengineer and Researcher specialized in Rehabilitation Engineering, Serious Games, and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI).

My research focuses on designing adaptive virtual environments, motor-cognitive interfaces (stereoscopic systems, treadmills, sensors), and Dynamic Difficulty Adjustment (DDA) architectures tailored for neurorehabilitation, cognitive assessment, and healthy aging.

> TECH INVENTORY

• Software & Engines: C++, Unity (C#), OpenGL, ROS/ROS2, Python (Pandas, MediaPipe, OpenCV)
• Hardware & Sensing: Meta Quest 3, SenseGlove Nova 2, Kinect V2, SmartTreadmills, Stereoscopic Projection Systems
